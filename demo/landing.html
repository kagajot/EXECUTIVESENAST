Why it is different

Most apps rank first. We disqualify first.

Straight answers

Is this medical advice?

No. It is decision support for choosing everyday tools. It does not diagnose or treat anything, and it does not replace an occupational therapist.

Are the tools reviewed by clinicians?

The product is built so that no tool can be recommended until a clinician has reviewed it. The tools in the preview are unreviewed examples, so treat them as a demonstration of how matching works.

Is this rehab or fitness?

No. It is about compensating and adapting: the tool or workaround that makes the task doable today, not exercises to get you stronger.

What does it cost?

The preview is free to try, with no card and no account. Pricing has not been set.

What if I do not know my abilities?

Leave them as not assessed. The app then tells you which single question would unlock the most tools, and never assumes the answer is yes.
